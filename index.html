Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize an image?

Upload your image, choose Resize Only, select a preset or enter custom dimensions, then tap Resize Images.

Can I resize an image without changing its proportions?

Yes. Keep Original Aspect Ratio enabled and ImageTools will automatically calculate the matching dimension.

What image sizes can I choose?

ImageTools includes common presets for 1920, 1600, 1280, 1080 and 800 pixels wide, plus custom dimensions.

How do I convert JPG to WebP?

Upload your JPG image, choose Convert Format, select WebP as the output format and process the image.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. Transparent areas are placed on a white background because JPG does not support transparency.

Can I convert WebP to PNG?

Yes. ImageTools supports WebP to PNG conversion directly inside your browser.

Does conversion change image dimensions?

No. Convert Format mode keeps the original image dimensions.

How do I make an image smaller than 200 KB?

Choose Compress & Resize, select Target File Size, enter 200 KB and select JPG or WebP.

Can I crop an image before resizing it?

Yes. Choose Compress & Resize, select Crop Image, choose a crop ratio and adjust the crop area before processing.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Processing happens directly in your browser.

Can I process multiple images?

Yes. You can select multiple images or drag multiple images into the upload area.

Which formats can I export?

You can export images as JPG, PNG or WebP.
