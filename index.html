Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize an image?

Choose Resize Only, select a preset or enter custom dimensions, then tap Resize Images.

Can I resize an image without changing its proportions?

Yes. Keep original aspect ratio enabled and ImageTools will automatically calculate the other dimension.

What image sizes can I choose?

ImageTools currently provides 1920, 1600, 1280, 1080 and 800 pixel width presets, plus custom dimensions.

Can I control the quality when resizing?

Yes. Resize Only includes a Smart Resize quality setting so you can balance image quality and file size.

How do I convert JPG to WebP?

Choose Convert Format, select WebP as the output format and process your image.

How do I convert JPG to PNG?

Choose Convert Format, select PNG as the output format and process your JPG or JPEG image.

How do I convert WebP to PNG?

Choose Convert Format, select PNG as the output format and process your WebP image.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. PNG images can be converted to JPG. Transparent areas are rendered against a white background because JPG does not support transparency.

Can I convert WebP to JPG?

Yes. WebP images can be converted to JPG directly in your browser.

Does conversion change image dimensions?

No. Convert Format keeps the original image dimensions.

How do I make an image smaller than 200 KB?

Choose Compress & Resize, select Target File Size, enter 200 KB and process the image.

Can I compress an image to 1MB?

Yes. Open the Compress Image to 1MB tool, select your image, and ImageTools will use a 1 MB target file size.

Can I crop an image?

Yes. Choose Crop Image and select 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9. ImageTools automatically crops from the center.

Can I remove an image background?

Yes. Open the AI Background Remover to automatically remove the background and download the result as a transparent PNG.

Are my images uploaded?

No. ImageTools processes your images directly inside your browser.

Can I process multiple images?

Yes. You can select multiple JPG, PNG or WebP images and process them together.

Which formats can I export?

ImageTools supports JPG, PNG and WebP output.

Can I compress a PDF?

Yes. Open Compress PDF Online to reduce PDF file size directly in your browser using Basic, Balanced or Strong compression.

Are PDFs uploaded to a server?

The ImageTools PDF Compressor processes the PDF locally in your browser rather than uploading the original PDF to our server.

Can I merge multiple PDF files?

Yes. Open Merge PDF Online, select two or more PDF files, arrange them in the order you want, and tap Merge PDF Files.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. ImageTools PDF Merger processes your PDF files locally in your browser. Your original files are not uploaded to our server.

Can I split a PDF?

Yes. Open Split PDF Online, select the page numbers or page ranges you want to extract, and create a new PDF.

Can I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Yes. You can enter individual pages such as 1,3,5 or page ranges such as 1-3. You can also combine ranges and individual pages.

Are my PDF files uploaded when splitting?

No. ImageTools PDF Splitter processes your PDF locally in your browser. Your original PDF is not uploaded to our server.

Can I convert a multi-page PDF to JPG?

Yes. PDF to JPG converts each page of a PDF into a separate JPG image. You can download individual pages or all converted pages as a ZIP file.

Can I download all PDF pages as JPG images?

Yes. After conversion, you can download individual JPG pages or download all converted pages together as a ZIP file.

Are my PDF files uploaded when converting to JPG?

No. ImageTools PDF to JPG processes your PDF directly in your browser. Your original PDF is not uploaded to our server.

Can I convert a multi-page PDF to PNG?

Yes. PDF to PNG converts each page of a PDF into a separate PNG image. You can download individual pages or all converted pages as a ZIP file.

Can I download all PDF pages as PNG images?

Yes. After conversion, you can download individual PNG pages or download all converted pages together as a ZIP file.

Are my PDF files uploaded when converting to PNG?

No. ImageTools PDF to PNG processes your PDF directly in your browser. Your original PDF is not uploaded to our server.

Can I extract text from a PDF?

Yes. PDF to Text extracts selectable text from PDF files directly in your browser and lets you copy or download the extracted text.

Can I download extracted PDF text as a TXT file?

Yes. After extracting the text, you can copy it to your clipboard or download it as a plain TXT file.

Are my PDF files uploaded when extracting text?

No. ImageTools PDF to Text processes your PDF directly in your browser. Your original PDF is not uploaded to our server.

Can I convert a PDF to Word?

Yes. PDF to Word converts selectable text from PDF files into an editable Word DOCX document directly in your browser.

Can I edit the converted Word document?

Yes. The generated DOCX document contains the extracted PDF text and can be edited in Microsoft Word and other compatible word-processing applications.

Are my PDF files uploaded when converting to Word?

No. ImageTools PDF to Word processes your PDF directly in your browser. Your original PDF is not uploaded to our server.

Can scanned PDFs be converted to Word?

This version extracts text that already exists inside the PDF. Scanned or image-only PDFs require OCR.
