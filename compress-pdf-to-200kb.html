Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compress a PDF to exactly 200KB?

The final file size depends on the contents and structure of the PDF. Compression can reduce the file substantially, but an exact 200KB result cannot always be guaranteed.

Is the PDF compressor free?

Yes. ImageTools provides browser-based PDF compression without requiring an account.

Do I need to upload my PDF?

No. ImageTools is designed to process PDFs directly in your browser, helping keep your original document on your device.

Will compressing a PDF reduce quality?

Depending on the compression method and the contents of the PDF, some visual quality or image detail may be reduced. The amount varies from document to document.

Can I compress a large PDF to 200KB?

It depends on the PDF. Documents containing many images or scanned pages may require stronger compression, and some PDFs may not be able to reach 200KB while maintaining acceptable quality.
