How to Crop an Image

  1. Tap Crop an Image.
  2. Select your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  3. Choose a crop ratio such as 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9.
  4. Process the image.
  5. Download your cropped image.

Why Crop an Image?

Cropping removes unwanted areas of an image and helps you focus on the most important part of a photo.

You can use image cropping to prepare pictures for websites, social media, profiles, documents and other projects.

Crop Images in Your Browser

ImageTools processes your image directly inside your browser. Your original image does not need to be uploaded to our server.

This makes the cropper convenient for quickly preparing images while keeping your original files on your device.

Crop Ratios

ImageTools supports common crop proportions including:

  • 1:1 — square images
  • 4:3 — common photo proportion
  • 16:9 — widescreen images
  • Free — no fixed crop ratio
