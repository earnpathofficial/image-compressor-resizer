How to Split a PDF

  • Select a PDF file.
  • Enter the pages you want to extract.
  • Use page ranges such as 1-3 or individual pages such as 1,3,5.
  • Tap Split PDF.
  • Download your new PDF.

You can combine page ranges and individual pages in the same request. For example: 1-3, 5, 8-10.

If you need to combine the extracted pages with other PDF files, you can use Merge PDF Online. If the resulting PDF is too large, use Compress PDF Online.
