PDF to Word Converter

ImageTools PDF to Word converts selectable text from PDF files into editable Microsoft Word DOCX documents.

The conversion runs locally in your browser, so your original PDF does not need to be uploaded to a remote server.

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Select your PDF file.
  2. Tap Convert to Word.
  3. Wait for the text to be extracted.
  4. Download the generated DOCX file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF to Word converter free?

Yes. ImageTools PDF to Word is free to use.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is processed directly in your browser.

Can I edit the converted Word document?

Yes. The result is a standard DOCX document containing extracted PDF text that can be edited in compatible word-processing applications.

Can scanned PDFs be converted?

This version extracts text that already exists inside the PDF. Scanned or image-only PDFs require OCR.

Does the original PDF remain on the server?

No. Conversion is performed locally in your browser and the original PDF is not uploaded to ImageTools.
