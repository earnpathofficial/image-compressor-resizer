Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert a PDF to Word for free?

Yes. ImageTools PDF to Word is free to use and processes the PDF directly in your browser.

Does the PDF get uploaded?

No. The PDF is processed locally in your browser. The original PDF is not uploaded to the ImageTools server.

Will the Word document be editable?

Yes. Extracted PDF text is placed into an editable DOCX document.

Will the original PDF layout be preserved?

The first version focuses on extracting text into an editable Word document. Complex PDF layouts, columns, graphics and exact positioning may not be preserved exactly.

Can I convert a scanned PDF?

Scanned image-only PDFs require OCR because they do not contain selectable text. This version does not perform OCR.

Can I use PDF to Word on my phone?

Yes. The tool is designed to work in modern mobile browsers as well as desktop browsers.

What file format does the converter create?

The result is downloaded as a Microsoft Word DOCX document.
