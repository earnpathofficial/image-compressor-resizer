Merge PDF Online for Free

ImageTools PDF Merger lets you combine multiple PDF documents into one file directly in your browser. It is useful when you need to combine documents for applications, school work, business documents, email attachments, or online submissions.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Select two or more PDF files.
  2. Arrange the files in the order you want.
  3. Remove any PDF you do not need.
  4. Tap Merge PDF Files.
  5. Download your merged PDF.

Reorder your PDF files

You can move PDF files up or down before merging. The final PDF follows the order shown in the file list.

Is this PDF merger free?

Yes. ImageTools PDF Merger is free to use in your browser.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Your PDF files are processed locally in your browser. The original files are not uploaded to ImageTools servers.

Can I merge multiple PDF files?

Yes. You can select multiple PDF files and combine them into a single PDF document.
