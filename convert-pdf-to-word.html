DOCX is the modern Microsoft Word document format. ImageTools generates a Word-compatible DOCX file from the selectable text extracted from your PDF.

After conversion, you can open the document using Microsoft Word or another application that supports the DOCX format.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert PDF to Word for free?

Yes. The ImageTools PDF to Word converter generates a Word-compatible DOCX document.

Yes. The resulting DOCX file is designed to contain editable extracted text that can be opened and edited in compatible word-processing software.
